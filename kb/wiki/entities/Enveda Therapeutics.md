# Enveda Therapeutics

Host of the CASMI 2026 Kaggle competition. Published `enveda-180`, a 1.15M-spectrum dataset of synthetic, pharmaceutical-style compounds measured on the same Bruker timsTOF platform as the hidden test, plus `enveda-np-examples` (250 common natural products processed through exactly the same pipeline as the test spectra, released to help build a matching preprocessing workflow). Their data dominates the instrument-matched portion of the training set — and simultaneously creates the chemistry difference to the natural-product-like test ([[concepts/data/Source Libraries]]).
