# Enveda Therapeutics

Host of the CASMI 2026 Kaggle competition. Published `enveda-180`, a 1.15M-spectrum timsTOF dataset of synthetic drug-like screening compounds acquired on the same Bruker timsTOF platform as the hidden test, plus `enveda-np-examples` (250 common natural products through the same pipeline as the test spectra, released to help build a domain-adapted pipeline). Their data dominates the instrument-matched portion of train — and simultaneously creates the chemistry gap to the natural-product-like test ([[concepts/data/Source Libraries]]).
