# Summary — Rules, Timeline and Prizes

**Code-competition constraints (the decisive ones):**
- Submissions run as Kaggle **notebooks**; scoring requires: at most **9 hours** of run-time (CPU or GPU), **no internet access** at scoring time, and only freely and publicly available external data — including **pretrained models** — attached as Kaggle datasets.
- The entire solution (retrieval, scoring, structure generation) must therefore run offline within 9 hours; every external asset (the COCONUT extract, pretrained weights) must be packaged as a dataset. See [[concepts/solution/Submission Architecture]].

**External data and tools:** allowed if publicly available and equally accessible to all participants at no cost, subject to a host-applied "Reasonableness Standard" (excluding, for example, high-cost models or datasets). Winning solutions must be published under an open-source licence (with an exception for inputs carrying incompatible licences). Sharing code publicly on the competition's Kaggle forum is permitted and itself becomes open-source licensed.

**Submission limits:** 5 per day; one account per participant; merged teams are capped by the combined daily quota.

**Timeline (11:59 PM UTC):**
- Start: Sep 14, 2026
- Entry deadline and team-merger deadline: Dec 7, 2026
- Final submission deadline: **Dec 14, 2026**

**Prizes: $50,000 total** — 1st $16k, 2nd $12k, 3rd $9k, 4th $7k, 5th $6k.
